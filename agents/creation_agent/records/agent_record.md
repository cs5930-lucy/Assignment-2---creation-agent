## Self-reflection draft

The biggest lesson from this exercise is that a response can pass every structural check and still miss the point of the assignment. The first version explained RAG broadly, but it did not show a dated capability chain or explain how source problems could affect product claims. The revision made the history more useful for a management decision.

The contrast cases also helped clarify what context should change. RAG's creation history stays the same, but an internal creative concept and a technical product description need different levels of evidence and review. The missing-controls case is especially useful because it shows that an aggressive adoption posture cannot replace approved documents or reviewer ownership.

For my marketing work, I would evaluate AI assistance by whether the final claims remain traceable and accurate in both languages, together with any time saved. I would also compare RAG with a simpler search or template workflow. AI helped organize the analysis, but choosing the evidence standard and accepting the remaining uncertainty still require professional judgment.
